About

Michael Dolejš (*1992) is a graphic designer with a keen interest in exploring the dynamic overlaps between digital, print and communication be it websites, apps, logotypes or visual identity systems.

He graduated from Faculty of Arts of University of West Bohemia in 2017 and started working with Studio Najbrt in the same year. He recently became a partner.

Apart from freelancing for many clients, he also worked in-house with many companies such as Ableton, STRV or Cleevio.
