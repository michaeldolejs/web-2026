Last.fm redesign I’ve done as a part of my final bachelor project at university, where I wanted to work on large-scale product that would give me an opportunity and experience I could use in future. I picked Last.fm, a service I myself still use, and gave myself a goal to redesign the platform and give it a new, more modern look, that it didn’t have at the time of this project and that would be modular enough to work on all major platforms such as web, iPad, iPhone and Android. I also tried to experiment how the service works and what can be changed to make it more competitite with other streaming platforms which meant pivoting into being streaming service itself.
