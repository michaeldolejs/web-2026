The brand new Ableton Note mobile app was released last week, and I'm very excited to finally show bits and pieces of my work on this project, which I participated in as an intern in Berlin in 2016.

My task for 3 months was to build on the already created research and come up with basic UX structure, try different visual explorations and make a working prototype which was then presented to the wider company leadership for further development approval.

The whole process was very interesting and I'm very happy to see the app out after such a long time!

Here are a few samples from the first few phases of the concept that were discussed and further developed after leaving my internship. Thanks to everyone involved!
