Ambiente is not only a group of restaurants, but mainly an environment created by people with the same view of gastronomy. Over the course of more than twenty-five years of history, they have gathered know-how that they like to share in the spirit of their hospitality. Therefore, we replaced the previous purposeful austerity of Ambiente with stories, insights, and principles that would make many housewives (and the Lord) jump over the fence. They are complemented by clearly structured information pages with a picture index of restaurants as well as electronic versions of A* magazine. And if you're just looking for a lunch menu, you'll find it in the side menu - at least until the evening!
