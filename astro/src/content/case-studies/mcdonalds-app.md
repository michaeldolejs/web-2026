Teamed up with Cleevio and OMD for a brand new official McDonald's mobile application, that would be used everywhere in Czech Republic and which would replace the older app, that I also worked on previously.

Our goal was to create a simple user interface, that would be easily scalable and updatable in the future and would better showcase McDonald's services. Sadly for us, after a year, McDonald's replaced our app with one global app, which brings more controls for the users
