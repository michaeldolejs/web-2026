The Wolves of Wood, one of the most important financial and investment groups in Central Europe, have changed their logotype and the entire visual identity after thirty years. Now it corresponds with their self-confidence, stability and growth. The compact logotype is dominated by the red inscription WOOD supported by the sharp center of the letter W. The new color and Atyp typeface by the Suitcase type foundry modified especially for Wood give the brand energy and credibility.
