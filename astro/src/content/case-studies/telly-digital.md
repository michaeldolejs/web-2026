For the new incarnation of the television and the Internet provider Telly, in addition to the name, logo and visual style, we have prepared the overall direction of the digital presentation. Supplementing the identity-defined "wipe" effect, we created a system for product categories, defined the use of mockups on all devices on which Telly applications run, and created a set of abstract motifs for various uses.
