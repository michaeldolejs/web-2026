The Karlovy Vary International Film Festival, under the acronym KVIFF, is expanding its activities with the KVIFF.TV film platform, KVIFF Classics festival of digitally restored films, KVIFF.TV Park multifunctional space and other projects like KVIFF Talents, KVIFF Events and KVIFF Distribution.

We created a logo system for the entire KVIFF Group, based on a distinctive, strong logotype that allows for numerous subdivisions. The system is complemented by a variable visual style with pictograms, a colour palette and motion design, which of course made its way onto the kviff.tv website.
