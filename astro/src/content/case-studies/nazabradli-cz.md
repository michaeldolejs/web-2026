A new website follows in the steps of the last year’s redesign of the logo and especially the visual style by a combination of the Sabon Next and Helvetica Now typefaces. In addition to a clear presentation of the current program and other activities of the theater, its ensemble and history, the emphasis is put mainly on attractive photographs from its ornate productions.
