Slovakia has moved to Holešovice!

For the new Ambiente Skô restaurant, located in the Holešovice Market Hall, we created a simple logo inspired by the restaurant's Slovak roots and the architectural design by Josef Pleskot’s studio. The interior features bold colours and simple forms, with a large, colourful cabinet dominating the dining area.

The entire visual identity draws upon the cabinet’s layout and colour scheme. Individual graphic elements are arranged, alternated, and adapted according to the specific application – ranging from menus, patterns, and various printed materials to traditional blue ceramics and the website.
