Každá vteřina (translated as Every Second) is the new web magazine of the Hodinářství Bechyně, which becomes a guide in the world of watches, watchmakers, their stories and everything that this topic brings.

For this project, we created the design of the magazine, the logo and the entire visual style, which is infused into the pages of the website, as well as the initial outdoor campaign. The dark blue colour scheme is complemented by gold, pink and phosphor colours, and the whole style is expanded by themed illustrations in the same style.
