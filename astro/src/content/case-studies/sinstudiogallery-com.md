Jiří Šín has come up with a unique technology of fused glass Vitrum Vivum, which allows to create exceptional works. Together with Rony Plesl and other artists, they create art objects and push the boundaries of the glass industry.

We were also inspired by this technology for the creation of the logo and other works related to the development of the visual style and presentations of the glassworks and specific artists.

As part of this branding, small galleries have been created in various European countries to exhibit glass and art objects from SIN collections.
