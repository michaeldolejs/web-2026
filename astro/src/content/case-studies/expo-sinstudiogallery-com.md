A new a simple presentation website for Sin Studio Gallery exhibition platform, which represents the Czech Republic at the EXPO 2025 world exhibition in Osaka, Japan is based on the contrast between the purity of space and the experimental concept of levitating elements that represent all of the art objects exhibited in the Czech pavilon.
