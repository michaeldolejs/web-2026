The Lucerna Music Bar website is changing two years after the logo and visual style change. Strong black-and-white typography helps clarity on a variety of devices even after several cocktails, the pages of individual events follow the system of posters in the streets, but otherwise the site using optional color filters and other effects indicates the energy of the experience of the club itself. We will meet in the first row (or in the toilet queue).
